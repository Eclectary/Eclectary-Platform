# Demo product image provenance

Prepared on 2026-11-16 for Eclectary demo product cards. Each source page was opened and checked against the approved photograph before download. The working source was downloaded from that page's original Unsplash or Pexels image host, converted to a 640 × 640 PNG, and cropped or padded without changing the depicted product.

## Processing and verification

- Original downloads were retained only in temporary working storage and were not added to the repository.
- EXIF orientation was applied before resizing.
- Every output is a true-color PNG at 640 × 640 pixels.
- Crops were visually checked after conversion to confirm the approved subject remained recognizable and undistorted.
- No product records, seed data, Active Storage attachments, or database state were changed during this preparation phase.
